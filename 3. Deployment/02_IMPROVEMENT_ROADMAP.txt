### 1.1 Fix Global Variables (CRITICAL)
**Difficulty**: Medium  
**Estimated Time**: 4-6 hours  
**Resume Value**: 5/5  
**MCA Project Value**: 5/5  
**Dependencies**: None  
**Risk of Breaking**: High  

**Description**: Replace global variables (`name`, `position`, `prev_q`, `flag`, `feedback`, `pace`) with session-based or database storage. This is critical for multi-user support.

**Implementation**:
